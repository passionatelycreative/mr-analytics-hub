Before the first chart

A little preparation goes a long way.

  • One row, one observation. Keep your dataset's structure consistent, whether each row is a response, event, or customer.
  • Give columns clear names. A single header row makes it easier to understand measures and categories.
  • Know what a value means. Record units, time periods, and any codes used for missing data.
  • Start with a specific question. “Which regions have the most missing responses?” is easier to investigate than “What does this mean?”
