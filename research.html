Build a stronger evidence trail

Three habits for useful research.

01

Record where it came from

Keep the source, collection date, sample definition, and any usage limits alongside each dataset. An interesting number needs context.

02

Check before you compare

Look for missing values, repeated records, inconsistent categories, and changes in definitions between groups or time periods.

03

Separate findings from guesses

State what the data shows, explain uncertainty, and keep possible explanations separate from the evidence you actually observed.
